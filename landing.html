How it works

Three steps to better hiring

1

Paste your Job Description

Sentinel reads your JD and generates tailored interview questions — Screening or In-depth — specific to the role and company.

2

Send the link to your candidate

The candidate receives a unique assessment link. They answer interview questions independently, with integrity monitoring active.

3

Receive the report by email

When the candidate finishes, a detailed report lands in your inbox automatically — scores, strengths, gaps, and a clear recommendation.

Features

Everything you need to assess candidates fairly

🎯

Dynamic questions from your JD

Every assessment is unique. Questions are generated from your specific job description and company context — not generic templates.

🤖

Adaptive follow-up questions

Sentinel listens. Follow-up questions are generated based on each candidate's actual answers — probing deeper where needed.

📊

Detailed scoring with custom weights

Four scoring categories — Technical Depth, Strategic Thinking, Communication, Cross-Functional — with weights you define per role.

🛡️

Integrity monitoring

Paste events, tab switches, and response time are tracked. You define the penalty — from informational-only to strict enforcement.

📋

Experience Fit vs Performance Score

Two separate scores: how well the candidate's background matches the role, and how well they actually performed in the interview.

📧

Automatic report delivery

No need to log in. The moment a candidate finishes, their full report arrives in your inbox — ready to review and share.
